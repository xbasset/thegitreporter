OpenCode can prepare a summary before context fills

Snapshot as of 2026-10-23. Monitoring counts and recommendations refer to that date; Pulse refreshes independently of articles.

OpenCode has merged experimental background compaction: it can prepare a conversation summary while the current conversation continues, then use it when compaction becomes due. The option remains off by default.

One decision-worthy insight. Action, effort, and rollback. 29 / 29 included projects monitored. Methodology ›

Today's Action

Watch / Project-specific claim
watch

OpenCode can prepare a summary before context fills

What changed. The implementation keeps the summary aside until needed, preserving the current conversation prefix. It checks that the summary still fits the conversation and can fall back to ordinary compaction. If the summary is unfinished, compaction waits for it; unused background work still consumes tokens.

Why it matters. For long coding sessions, preparing work early offers a different way to manage the compaction pause. Its value depends on whether the prepared summary is used and preserves the information needed to finish the task.
