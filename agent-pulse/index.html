watch

Check the receiver before relying on agent status lights

What changed. Crush prioritizes permission, question and authentication dialogs over busy state. Pi reports the outcome after its session settles and distinguishes cancellation from completion. The terminal chooses how to display the structured reports.

Why it matters. People working across several agent tabs gain a common signal for which one needs a response. Actual usefulness depends on receiver support and the path through any terminal multiplexer.

Check the complete sender and receiver path

Before relying on status indicators, check that the installed terminal supports OSC 7501 and that any intermediary forwards it. In a disposable task, observe a question, cancellation and completion, then check that the indicator clears. Keep normal conversation review until that path is demonstrated.
